學習目標（Learning objectives）

  • 能區分 master unit、ancillary unit 與純化編碼的作用。（Distinguish master units, ancillary units, and code purification.）
  • 能用有限樣本及嚴格進展解釋停止論證。（Explain finite termination through strict progress on a finite sample set.）
  • 能說明訓練全對不保證泛化，也不保證小網路。（Explain why zero training error guarantees neither generalization nor a small network.）

KP05 依錯誤需求逐步增長網路（Grow a network in response to remaining classification needs）

一句話重點：構造式方法從小架構開始，依規則增加節點或層，讓有限訓練集逐步可分；這是訓練集上的目標，不等於對未見資料的保證。

One-sentence summary: Constructive methods start small and add nodes or layers under rules that improve separability of a finite training set. Their training-set objective does not guarantee performance on unseen data.

從已知幾何答案轉向只有訓練點（From known geometry to labeled training points）

白話說明：KP04 的存在性構造知道目標區域，實務卻通常只有點與標籤。構造式方法先用很小的網路嘗試，再依尚未解決的部分加節點。有些方法增加深度，有些固定深度增加寬度。

Plain explanation: KP04 assumed known target regions, while applications usually supply points and labels. A constructive method starts with a small network and adds capacity where classification remains unresolved. Some variants grow depth; others keep depth fixed and grow width.

可把過程想成先畫少量隔板，再處理仍混雜的區域。不過新增節點的方式必須有規則；隨便增加神經元，並不自動得到有限步停止的保證。

Think of drawing a few dividers, then addressing the remaining mixed cells. Node additions must follow specified rules; arbitrary growth does not automatically guarantee finite termination.

Tiling 的兩種節點各負責什麼（The two roles in tiling）

正式定義：設有限標記訓練集為 X={(xn,cn)}n=1NX=\{(\mathbf{x}_n,c_n)\}_{n=1}^NX={(xn​,cn​)}n=1N​，類別 cn∈{0,1}c_n\in\{0,1\}cn​∈{0,1}。第一個主要分類節點稱 master unit，以 pocket 等線性學習方法在該層輸入上訓練。

Formal definition: Let X={(xn,cn)}n=1NX=\{(\mathbf{x}_n,c_n)\}_{n=1}^NX={(xn​,cn​)}n=1N​ be a finite labeled set with binary labels. A master unit is the main classifier trained on that layer's inputs, for example with a pocket-based linear learner.

master 的輸出把資料分成 X+X^+X+ 與 X−X^-X−。若同一輸出群仍含兩類，增加 ancillary unit，只用需要分辨的混雜子集訓練它。新增位元的目的，是讓不同類樣本不再共享完全相同的層輸出。

The master partitions inputs into X+X^+X+ and X−X^-X−. If one output group contains both classes, add an ancillary unit trained on the relevant mixed subset. Its new bit helps prevent opposite-label samples from sharing the complete layer code.

有限步停止究竟依賴什麼（What finite termination actually requires）

第一種進展是，同層處理混雜子集時，需繼續分辨的子問題嚴格變小。第二種進展是，來源所述的適當層間構造，讓新 master 保留原來分對的點，並至少多分對一點。兩個論證不能混成「每加一個節點，總錯誤一定立刻下降」。

One progress argument shrinks the mixed subgroup that still needs refinement within a layer. A second, under the source's appropriate inter-layer construction, preserves the earlier master's correct classifications and adds at least one. Neither says that every arbitrary new node immediately lowers total error.

若第 kkk 層 master 正確點數為 CkC_kCk​，上述保留與進步條件可寫成 Ck+1≥Ck+1C_{k+1}\geq C_k+1Ck+1​≥Ck​+1，且 Ck≤NC_k\leq NCk​≤N。有限整數序列不可能永遠嚴格增加。這是條件成立時的停止理由，不是宣稱 pocket 在任何不可分子集上都會找到完美直線。

Let CkC_kCk​ count the correctly classified points at master layer kkk. The preservation-and-progress condition is Ck+1≥Ck+1C_{k+1}\geq C_k+1Ck+1​≥Ck​+1, with Ck≤NC_k\leq NCk​≤N. A bounded integer sequence cannot increase forever. This is conditional termination reasoning, not a claim that pocket perfectly separates every nonseparable subgroup.

［補充］必要資料條件也要說明：如果完全相同的輸入被標成兩個互斥類別，任何確定性網路都不可能全對。有限步精確分類的敘述必須排除這種標籤衝突，並滿足構造所要求的嚴格進展。

[Added] Identical inputs with contradictory labels cannot both be correctly classified by a deterministic network. Exact finite classification requires excluding such conflicts and satisfying the construction's strict-progress conditions.

三層足夠，與演算法最多長三層不同（Three layers suffice does not cap every growth procedure）

第一層之後的輸入是二元碼，因此可以使用 KP04 的頂點偵測與 OR 構造。這說明適當的三層表示足以完成有限純化碼的分類，但不表示任何逐層增長的 tiling 執行都自動在三層內停止。

Later-layer inputs are binary codes, so KP04's detector-and-OR construction can classify them. This establishes sufficiency of a suitable three-layer representation, not an automatic three-layer stopping bound for every layer-growing tiling execution.

來源把這兩種說法接得過緊；這裡明確區分存在性架構與特定訓練程序的深度保證，不把它們當成同一個定理。

The source connects these statements too closely. We distinguish a sufficient architecture from a depth guarantee for a particular training procedure.

訓練全對不代表學得好（Perfect training classification is not the whole goal）

常見迷思：既然最後能把訓練點全分對，就保證新資料也正確。保證只涉及已提供的有限樣本；網路可能為這些點建立過多局部區域，對未見位置卻沒有好預測。

Common misconception: Perfect training classification does not guarantee correct predictions on new inputs. The guarantee concerns supplied finite samples; a network can create many local cells without predicting unseen locations well.

同樣地，有限步停止不代表網路最小、計算最省，或所有學習選擇都同樣可靠。這些落差引出另一條主流路線：先固定架構，再以代價函數學習權重，亦即下一批的反向傳播。

Finite termination also guarantees neither minimal size nor low computation, and not every learning choice is equally reliable. These gaps motivate the next route: fix the architecture and learn weights by minimizing a cost function through backpropagation.
